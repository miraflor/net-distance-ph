Method

Distances are population-weighted conditional means of shortest modeled road-and-ferry paths between representative sites. Blank CSV cells are +∞ in the NumPy matrix: no modeled reachable population pairs.

The map's roads and ferry lines are for geographic context only; they are not necessarily the routes used for each origin-destination calculation.
